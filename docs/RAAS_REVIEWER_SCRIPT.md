# 90-second RaaS reviewer walkthrough

This script is designed for a short screen recording of
[`examples/raas-deal-room.html`](../examples/raas-deal-room.html).

## 0 to 15 seconds

Open the decision room.

Say:

> This is a synthetic cross-border Robotics-as-a-Service deal. The first screen
> answers the management question directly. The current draft is blocked, with the
> number of blocking items, required approvers, and open regulatory controls visible.

## 15 to 35 seconds

Scroll through the signing blockers.

Say:

> The system applies deterministic negotiation guardrails to liability, acceptance,
> IP, data and model improvement, safety responsibilities, exclusivity, service
> levels, termination, cybersecurity, and hardware risk. Every issue links back to a
> structured synthetic fact and names the accountable approvers.

## 35 to 55 seconds

Show the deployment and revenue delay panels.

Say:

> Legal is integrated with Operations and Finance. The pack separates deployment
> blockers from revenue questions, including objective acceptance, service credits,
> termination rights, performance obligations, and the possible lease assessment.

## 55 to 75 seconds

Show the regulatory matrix.

Say:

> The readiness matrix distinguishes the relevant actor and evidence for machinery,
> the AI Act, the Data Act, cybersecurity, product liability, and GDPR. The sources
> are primary EU materials, dated and hashed for review.

## 75 to 90 seconds

Open the Markdown pack or case study.

Say:

> The same run also produces external-counsel instruction packs and a founding
> General Counsel 100-day plan. Nothing sends, signs, or approves itself. The value is
> a legal function whose judgment is structured, testable, and visible to management.
