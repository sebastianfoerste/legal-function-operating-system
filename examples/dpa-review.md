# DPA clause review (Art. 28(3) GDPR / DSGVO)

- **2** documents · **1** blocker (missing requirements)
- `pass`: every statutory element of the requirement was found. `review`: an element is missing, or its wording is negated; the column lists which. `missing`: no clause. Art. 28 Abs. 3 lit. a to h block when missing; the UAbs. 2 row is marked for review instead.
- The review states coverage of the statutory wording, not that an agreement is lawful. Human review is required.
