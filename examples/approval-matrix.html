Approval matrix

Policy: Default illustrative policy. The chain names who must sign off before a request can close. Every chain starts with a human reviewer, and the matrix approves nothing. Nothing here is legal advice.

Who is added to the chain

  1. Every request: Reviewer
  2. Value band "50k-250k" or above: Legal Ops Lead
  3. Value band "250k-1m" or above, or HIGH risk: General Counsel
  4. Value band ">1m" or above: Board note

What sets the risk

  1. HIGH if any of: Personal data transferred outside the EEA; Value band ">1m" or above; Uncapped liability; A dispute at value band "250k-1m" or above.
  2. MEDIUM if any of: Value band "250k-1m" or above; Personal data processed; Request type corporate, fundraising, ai_governance.
  3. LOW otherwise.
